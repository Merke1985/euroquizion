✨ The Glitter Tower

Stella, the keeper of the tower, shows each climber the next ledge to jump to. Every floor has just two ledges: one straight above you and one beside it (up and to the left or right). Only one is real.

On your turn, tap ↖ ↑ ↗ on your phone as fast as you can. The wrong ledge crumbles, and taking too long makes you slip: either way you fall 3 floors. The higher you get, the less time you have.

Everyone gets 3 turns of 15 seconds (with many players, 2 or 3 climb at once). Highest floor at the end wins.
