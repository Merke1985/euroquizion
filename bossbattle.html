⚔️ Boss Battle

A second kind of Grand Final: everyone together against the monster. Above its head you see what it’s about to do (its intent), so you can plan.

Every turn you get 3 cards. Play one: you get a question, and if you answer it right, the card works. Harder questions do more. When your health runs out you become a backup dancer: you can still cheer the others on (heal them).

⚔️ Strike: easy · 6 damage🎤 Power Ballad: hard · 15 damage🛡️ Sequin Shield: easy · block 8💞 Duet: medium · block 6 for a teammate✨ Encore: medium · heal 6🎹 Key Change: hard · your next attack ×2

Beat the monster within 10 turns and the team wins: the trophy goes to whoever did the most (damage, plus half of the shielding and healing).
