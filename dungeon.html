⚔️ The Guardian's Vault

Deep in a dungeon a stone Guardian watches over three chests: gold, silver and bronze. Only one holds the treasure. Every chest carries a riddle.

  • At least one chest tells only the truth.
  • At least one chest only lies.
  • A chest with nothing on it neither lies nor tells the truth; a chest with two lines may mix one true and one false.

Work out which chest holds the treasure and pick it on your phone (you can change your mind until time runs out). Then the Guardian smashes the other two. Wrong chest: you lose a ❤️. Everyone has 2 lives. Last ones standing win.
