🐦‍⬛ Blackbird

The whole flock flies along with the song. Most of the time your bird is on autopilot and can’t crash.

Every few seconds a few birds are picked at random to fly themselves. When it’s you: hold to fly up, let go to glide down, and stay inside the band of light, away from the branches.

A hit costs a feather 🪶 (you have 3). No feathers left and your bird lands on a branch. Most feathers when the song ends wins.
