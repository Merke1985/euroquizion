🐦‍⬛ Blackbird

The whole flock takes off from a rooftop, flies through the night sky and then down into a dark forest, along with the song. Most of the time every bird is on autopilot and can’t crash.

When the flock is up, everyone flies at the same time, each with their own phone: hold to fly up, let go to glide down. Over the pine forest only the trees are in the way; in the cave keep to the path of fireflies, away from the leaves above, the undergrowth below and the branches.

A hit costs a feather 🪶 (you have 5). No feathers left and your bird lands on a branch. Most feathers when the song ends wins.
