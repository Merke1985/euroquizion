💞 Odd Couples

You play in pairs. A long Eurovision song title appears on the big screen. Does it have an even or an odd number of words?

In every pair one partner has the EVEN button and the other the ODD button, so only the right partner can answer. Decide together who presses! A wrong press locks your pair out for that round. Now and then the buttons swap.

First correct pair: 3 points. Every other correct pair: 1 point. 10 rounds.
