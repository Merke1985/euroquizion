EuroQuizion!

The Eurovision party quiz for 2 to 12 players: guess the songs, play party games, sabotage your friends and race to the trophy!

How to play

  1. 📺 One screen for everyone. One person taps “Host a game” on a TV or laptop that everyone can see. It plays the songs and shows the show.
  2. 📱 Join with your phone. Everyone else scans the QR code or enters the room code here, picks a name, an avatar and the country they represent.
  3. 🎶 Guess the songs. Answer the questions about Eurovision songs on your phone. Questions about your own country count double!
  4. 🎉 Party games and the Grand Final. Win party games to go shopping in the boutique, use your items to sabotage your friends, and race to the trophy in the Grand Final!
